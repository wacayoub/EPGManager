EPGManager 2026.11.12-rc83
==========================

Fixes
- A channel counts as "written" only when it received at least one programme
  that has not ended yet. Before, a stale feed (only finished programmes) still
  marked the channel as owned by EPGManager, which blocked its satellite EIT and
  left the guide blank. Seen on the receiver: AD Sport 1/2, Star Movies, Bloomberg,
  Nat Geo Wild, DreamWorks, Al Hayat and others.
- Channels whose feed had only finished programmes get their satellite guide back
  at the end of the same import (no 48-hour wait).

Speed (import plan; same plan as rc82, verified on the receiver)
- Duplicate-channel name keys are memoised (was ~200k recomputations).
- Source ranking caches the source-only part and the name/provider bonus.
- The "dead feed" check is skipped for sources that cannot have a verdict
  (no GitHub monitor entry and no local quality profile).
- The shared-Rytec-map test is cached per source pair.
