rc82 builds on rc81.
SAT EIT returns to a satellite service after 48 hours without EPGManager events.
rc81 kept SAT EIT off on every service it had ever imported, so a channel whose
feed stopped covering it kept an empty guide (seen on Al Jazeera English HD and
Al Jazeera Documentary). Upgrading from rc81 releases nothing at once: existing
services start their 48-hour count at the first rc82 import.
EPG Import source files no longer point at /tmp, which is cleared on reboot.
The log is written to /media/hdd/epgmanager/logs when an HDD is mounted, so it
survives reboots and crashes.
