EPGManager 8.2.2-rc34 — Modern AI SaaS + Direct EPG-Scrapers

- New modern AI/PHP-SaaS visual palette: deep navy surfaces, violet primary accent, cyan secondary accent, cleaner cards and selected rows.
- Smart Sources now has a dedicated EPG SCRAPERS provider page.
- Adds validated direct GitHub feeds from wacayoub/EPG-Scrapers:
  * Direct 2M
  * Direct Chada TV
  * Direct beIN MENA
  * Direct ElCinema Arabic
  * Direct OSN
  * Direct Sport24
- Direct feeds point to feeds/<source>.xml.gz and retain independent source IDs; no XML merge is introduced.
- beIN, ElCinema, OSN and Sport24 use their published feeds/<source>.txt channel-ID catalogues when available.
- Removes all Arabic/MENA OpenEPG feeds from Smart Sources (Morocco, Egypt, Palestine, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE).
- Removes the remaining Arabic EPGShare source (Al Jazeera); EPGShare is now international/non-Arab fallback only.
- Existing invalid persisted selections are automatically cleaned by SourcePreferences on catalogue reload.
- OpenEPG non-Arab feeds (Turkey, France, Spain) and Rytec/Azman/Koala remain available.
- Daily direct-source schedule metadata: 06:00 Africa/Casablanca.
