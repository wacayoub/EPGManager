EPGManager 2026.11.14-rc87
==========================

Time shown clearly for users in every time zone
- Settings: the automatic import time is UTC. The row now says so and shows
  the receiver's offset, e.g. "EPG Import start time (UTC; local UTC+1)",
  and the help panel converts the chosen time: "13:15 UTC = 14:15 on this
  receiver". "GMT+0" alone was easy to read as local time.
- System Check adds RECEIVER TIME ZONE (information only, never a warning):
  the zone and offset of the receiver clock, with a hint when the box runs on
  UTC that clock, guide and timers then show UTC.
- Home screen TIME / SYSTEM shows the receiver zone ("UTC", "UTC+1") until
  the live NOW comparison has samples, instead of "UNKNOWN".

Unchanged
- Import, mapping and guide-strength logic are identical to rc86. The import
  time keeps its UTC meaning, so existing settings do not move.
